About this portal

The SOC Portal is where the Landasan Digital security operations team tracks incidents, follows playbooks and reports on its work.

Who runs it

Landasan Digital is a managed security services provider (MSSP) based in Shah Alam, Selangor. Our security operations centre (SOC) watches for threats around the clock and responds when something goes wrong.

What you can do here

  • See and update open incidents (SOC Analyst role).
  • Report an incident and get a reference number.
  • Read and write shift handovers (SOC Analyst role).
  • Keep the watchlist of indicators to block or monitor (SOC Analyst role).
  • Follow a playbook step by step.
  • Check who to tell, and copy a message template.
  • See trends and resolved incidents.

Where the data lives

Incidents, handovers, the watchlist, messages and help articles are stored in Microsoft Dataverse in our own environment. Access is controlled by portal roles: you need the SOC Analyst role to see the incident list. Check your roles on Profile and access.
