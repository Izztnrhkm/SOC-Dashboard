Questions and answers

What counts as a P1 critical incident?

An active breach that is not contained and affects core systems or confidential data. For example: ransomware encrypting files across the network, a domain controller taken over, or customer data being copied out.

P1 incidents get a response within 15 minutes and the CISO is told straight away. See all severity levels.

A device shows a ransom message. What do I do first?

Do not switch it off or restart it: that destroys evidence we need. Unplug the network cable or turn off Wi-Fi, leave the device on, and call the hotline on +60 3-5524 3133.

How do I report an incident?

Use the report form or the red Report incident button at the top of every page. It takes about a minute and you get a reference number. For P1 or P2, call the hotline first.

How do I report a phishing email?

Use the Report button in Outlook. If you can’t, save the email as an .eml or .msg file and send it through the contact form. Don’t forward it: forwarding removes the headers we need.

If you clicked a link or typed your password, call the hotline now.

Why was my device cut off from the network?

Our endpoint protection (EDR) isolates a device when it sees signs of an attack. The device can still talk to the security tools, but nothing else. An analyst will look at it and release it when it is safe. To ask about it, call the hotline or use the contact form.

How are indicators checked before they are blocked?

An analyst checks each IP address, domain and file hash against threat intelligence before adding it to the firewall and EDR block lists, so we don’t block something legitimate by mistake.

How do I get access to the incident dashboard?

Your portal account needs the SOC Analyst role. Ask a portal admin, or send a request with the contact form. You can check your roles on Profile and access.

How do I reset my password or unlock my account?

Use self-service password reset first. If your MFA is locked, call the service desk and be ready to confirm who you are. The SOC will never ask for your password.

What goes in a shift handover?

Three things: what happened on your shift, what is still open, and what the next shift should keep an eye on. On the Handover page, the Add open incidents link copies the current open incidents into the form for you.

When should something go on the watchlist?

Add an indicator when you have confirmed it is malicious (Block) or suspicious enough to alert on (Monitor). Always give a reason, and set an expiry date unless the indicator should stay blocked for good, so the list stays short and accurate. See the Watchlist.

Who can see the incidents I report?

Everyone with the SOC Analyst role. Other staff can report incidents but cannot see the incident list.
