Severity levels

Pick the highest level that fits. When unsure, go higher. Click a row to pre-select it in the report form.

LevelResponseWhat it meansExamples
P1 Critical15 minActive, uncontained breach hitting core operations or customer data.Ransomware encrypting, domain controller compromised, command-and-control on a payment server.
P2 High30 minSevere risk: privileged account compromise or lateral movement.Credential dump, password spray on admin portal, rogue scheduled task.
P3 Medium2 hoursModerate risk, usually contained.Adware on an endpoint, suspicious PowerShell blocked by AMSI.
P4 Low8 hoursInformational or minor anomaly.Port scan on the edge, unpatched non-critical CVE.

Response lifecycle

Based on NIST SP 800-61.

PrepareHardened sensors, baselined telemetry, offline backups.
Detect & triageCorrelate alerts, map the technique, set severity and scope.
ContainIsolate hosts, revoke sessions, block bad indicators.
Eradicate & recoverRemove persistence, rebuild from known-good, restore service.
LearnRoot cause review within five days; update detections and playbooks.

Playbooks

Tick steps off as you go. Your progress is kept on this device.

P1Ransomware and lateral movementPre-execution, shadow copy deletion or active encryption0 of 6 done
Cut off SMB, WMI and WinRM movement while keeping sensor telemetry flowing.
Invoke-EDRIsolation -Host FIN-DB-SVR01 -EnforceMode StrictlyIsolated
Dump volatile memory of injected processes (rundll32, dllhost, powershell) before terminating them.
Dump-VolatileMemory -PID 4192 -Destination \\forensics-vault\evidence\
Powering down destroys the memory artifacts needed for decryption keys and attribution.
Confirm backup repositories were not touched. Lock the storage bucket read-only.
Push C2 IPs, domains and hashes to the firewall and EDR blocklists.
Call the hotline, brief the CISO and open a P1 bridge.
P2Business email compromise and identity takeoverHijacked M365 or Entra ID account, rogue forwarding rules0 of 5 done
Sign the user out everywhere.
Revoke-AzureADUserAllRefreshToken -ObjectId "user-guid"
Do this after sessions are revoked, not before.
Attackers often add rules that forward or hide replies.
Get-InboxRule -Mailbox user@cyberguard.sec | Remove-InboxRule
Remove unknown app consents and note the attacker IP addresses.
Treat any recent payment-detail change as suspect until verified by phone.
P3Phishing email reported by a userLink clicked, attachment opened or credentials entered0 of 5 done
Ask for the .eml or .msg file. Forwarding strips the headers we need.
Find every copy by sender or subject and soft-delete it.
New-ComplianceSearch -Name PhishPurge -ExchangeLocation All -ContentMatchQuery 'from:bad@example.com'
Add them to Defender for Office 365 and the web proxy.
Use URL click traces and sign-in logs. Treat clickers as possibly compromised.
Follow the identity takeover playbook if anyone submitted a password.

Report an incident

Please describe what happened in a few words.

While you wait

  • Do not turn the device off.
  • Disconnect it from the network (cable or Wi-Fi).
  • Do not delete anything, including the suspicious email.
  • Write down what you clicked and when.

You will get a reference such as INC-2026-8950 to quote on the phone.
