Severity levels

Pick the highest level that fits. When unsure, go higher.

LevelResponse timeWhat it meansExamples
P1 Critical15 minActive, uncontained breach hitting core operations or customer data.Ransomware encrypting, domain controller compromised, command-and-control on a payment server.
P2 High30 minSevere risk: privileged account compromise or lateral movement.Credential dump, password spray on admin portal, rogue scheduled task.
P3 Medium2 hoursModerate risk, usually contained.Adware on an endpoint, suspicious PowerShell blocked by AMSI.
P4 Low8 hoursInformational or minor anomaly.Port scan on the edge, unpatched non-critical CVE.

Response lifecycle

Based on NIST SP 800-61.

PrepareHardened sensors, baselined telemetry, offline backups.
Detect & triageCorrelate alerts, map the technique, set severity and scope.
ContainIsolate hosts, revoke sessions, block bad IPs at the edge.
Eradicate & recoverRemove persistence, rebuild systems, restore clean data.
ReviewPublish root cause, close detection gaps, update playbooks.
