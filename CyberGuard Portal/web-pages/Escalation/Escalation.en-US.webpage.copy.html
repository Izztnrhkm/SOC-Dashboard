Legal deadlines in Malaysia

These apply to the organisation that owns the data or system, usually the client. Make sure the client knows in time to meet them, and involve their DPO or legal team.

Personal data breach

Personal Data Protection Act 2010, as amended. Since 1 June 2025.

72 hours
Notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner, counted from when the breach is known, if it is likely to cause significant harm or affects more than 1,000 people.
7 days
Within 7 days of notifying the Commissioner, notify the affected people if the breach is likely to cause them significant harm.

Significant harm includes financial loss, identity fraud, misuse for illegal purposes, physical harm, or sensitive personal data being exposed.

National critical infrastructure (NCII)

Cyber Security Act 2024 and its notification regulations.

Immediately
Notify NACSA and the sector lead when an incident is found.
6 hours
Send the first report with the incident details.
14 days
Send the full report: scope, impact, cause and actions.

Only for clients designated as NCII entities.

A summary for the SOC, not legal advice. Check with legal before any notice is sent.

Message templates

Copy, fill in the parts in square brackets, and send. Keep it factual.

Internal alert (Teams or chat)Send to the SOC channel the moment a P1 or P2 is confirmed.
[P1/P2] [INC reference]: [one-line title]
What: [what we see, in one sentence]
Affected: [hosts / accounts / client]
Status: [investigating / contained]
Lead: [name]  Bridge: [link or number]
Next update: [time]
